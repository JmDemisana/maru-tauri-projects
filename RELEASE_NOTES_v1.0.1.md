### 🎹 Monika Piano Maker v1.0.1

A maintenance and bugfix release for Monika Piano Maker.

#### 🛠️ Changes & Fixes in v1.0.1
- **Time Mode Tapper Retiming Fix**: Fixed an issue where the final note of a phrase was left uncalibrated.
- **Intelligent Cadence Hold**: Automatically allocates natural trailing hold duration to phrase finales and smoothly bridges inter-phrase tempo transitions.
- **In-Place Upgrade Support**: Upgrades existing `v1.0.0` installations seamlessly without requiring an uninstall or reconfiguring shortcuts.

- **Windows Setup Installer (Recommended)**: `Monika Piano Maker_1.0.1_x64-setup.exe`
- **Windows MSI Package**: `Monika Piano Maker_1.0.1_x64_en-US.msi`
- **Portable Standalone Executable**: `monika-piano-maker.exe`
